01 / THE INTENTION

Give the object
a presence.

The brief was self-directed: create a distinctive audio-commerce experience that balances the atmosphere of a product film with the usefulness of a storefront.

The result centres on an original procedural headphone model. Material, silhouette and light tell the story. Scroll-directed camera movement introduces the object; manual controls let a visitor look closer at their own pace.

FORMA is a fictional brand. The project demonstrates design and implementation decisions, with no client commission, sales results or real product specifications claimed.

03 / THE IMPLEMENTATION

The details behind
the first impression.

A considered surface needs a dependable foundation. The commerce logic is kept separate from the visual experience.

10 tests

The automated cart suite covers quantities, delivery thresholds, invalid data, storage input and sample-order export.

Integer cents

Line items and totals use whole currency cents. Quantities and bundle names are validated before calculations.

Local only

Browser storage retains a sample cart. No account, real payment or customer information is required.

04 / THE HANDOFF

A complete concept.
An honest scope.

The deliverable includes buildless HTML, CSS and JavaScript, locally bundled Three.js, the custom 3D model, responsive layouts and a separately tested cart module. Manual controls, reduced-motion support and a static image fallback are part of the experience.

The cart and JSON export are demonstrations. They make the interaction tangible without processing a transaction.

PRODUCTION BOUNDARYThis is a fictional product demonstration. Payments, inventory, tax integration, fulfilment, customer accounts and transactional email are outside its scope. A commercial launch would require those systems and verified product information.
