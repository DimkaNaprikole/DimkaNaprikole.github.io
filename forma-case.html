01 / THE DIRECTION

A cabinet of
listening curiosities.

The creative brief pairs an expressive steampunk aesthetic with the clarity of a contemporary storefront. Warm metal, dark surfaces and sculptural objects establish the atmosphere; product information and navigation remain easy to find.

Three headphones, three speakers and three record-player concepts give the collection variety. Each object has its own silhouette, finish and illustrative price. Original AI-generated concept imagery supports the art direction; it does not depict verified manufactured products.

02 / THE EXPERIENCE

Discover the object.
Explore the details.

Explore all nine concepts ↗
  1. 01 / FIND

    A collection that is easy to explore.

    Category filters and text search narrow the nine objects. A clear empty state helps visitors recover when a search has no results.

  2. 02 / INSPECT

    One object, considered closely.

    Clicking a product winds up a full-screen movement of 32 textured gears. The object emerges after 2.5 seconds while the mechanism keeps turning behind it. Category changes stay immediate; reduced-motion preferences skip the effect. Product details and the local bag remain fully usable.

  3. 03 / COLLECT

    A working local shopping bag.

    Mixed products, quantity changes, removal and delivery calculations stay connected. The sample bag can be restored after a browser reload.

  4. 04 / REVIEW

    A tangible demonstration.

    An order review and downloadable JSON sample show the complete interaction without collecting customer details or processing a payment.

03 / THE FOUNDATION

Expression on the surface.
Care in the details.

12 tests

The automated catalog and cart suite covers mixed products, quantity caps, delivery boundaries, invalid storage data and sample-order export.

Integer units

Prices and totals use whole minor units of fictional gold coins. Product identifiers and quantities are validated before they enter the cart calculations.

Local only

The demonstration keeps the bag in browser storage. It needs no account, payment card, address or connection to a checkout service.

04 / THE HANDOFF

A complete experience.
A clear scope.

Buildless HTML, CSS and JavaScript keep the interface portable. Product data, cart calculations and DOM interactions are separated. The standalone edition embeds its assets so the demo can also be opened as an HTML file.

This is an independently designed portfolio demonstration. No client commission, operational shop, acoustic performance, manufacturing feasibility, sale or measured business outcome is claimed.

PRODUCTION BOUNDARYAll prices are illustrative. Payments, verified specifications, inventory, taxes, fulfilment, customer accounts and transactional email would require separate production systems and real product information.
