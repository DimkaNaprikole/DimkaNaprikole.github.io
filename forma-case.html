01 / THE BRIEF

Make the choice
feel effortless.

The self-directed brief: launch a fictional premium audio product without overwhelming a visitor. Give the product space, explain the difference between two sets and make price and delivery visible before the next step.

The design uses warm paper tones, midnight titanium, editorial typography and an sculpted open-back 3D object study and supporting product imagery. No invented reviews, sales figures or conversion claims.

02 / THE EXPERIENCE

One continuous
decision path.

Try the configuration ↗
  1. Understand. A custom procedural 3D headphone model can be rotated and enlarged with pointer or keyboard controls. The scene pauses offscreen and respects reduced-motion preferences.
  2. Compare. Essential and Travel sets show their contents and prices together.
  3. Build. Quantities and cart totals update consistently. Delivery is calculated before review.
  4. Review. The bag survives reloads in the same browser. A clearly labelled sample order can be downloaded.

04 / THE HANDOFF

Clear scope.
Useful source.

The deliverable includes buildless HTML, CSS and JavaScript, a separately tested cart module, responsive layouts, locally bundled Three.js, a bespoke geometric headphone model, keyboard-operable scene controls and native dialogs, reduced-motion support and a README for handoff.

Production boundary
Real inventory, payment, tax, fulfilment, customer accounts and transactional email are not implemented. A production launch would require those integrations and real product information.
